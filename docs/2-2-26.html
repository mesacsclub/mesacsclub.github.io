Who are We?

We are the San Diego Mesa College Computer Science Club. We are a community of computer scientists, providing each-other with creative inspiration and a collaborative environment 🤝. Contact our members using the information above.

What are we Doing?

We hold meetings biweekly on Tuesdays and Fridays (typically 12pm-2pm) on Mesa College Campus.

History

We’ve been at Mesa since the start of Spring 2024, continually meeting through the end of Fall 2025. As a group, we’ve attended these events in and around San Diego:

  • Big Data Hackathon 2024 at SDSU
  • IEEE SD Section Student Networking Event Fall 2024 at Qualcomm
  • RoseHack 2025 at UCR
  • DiamondHacks 2025 at UCSD

We’ve hosted a few workshops, and have every intent to hold more student events.
